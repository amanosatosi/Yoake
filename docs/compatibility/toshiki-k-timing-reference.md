Toshiki maintains text slots and explicitly assigned audio boundaries separately. A new untimed line's first pending slot spans the remainder; later pending slots have no invented timing. Left-click assigns the next boundary or drags a nearby boundary, and earlier timed regions audition without advancing the cursor. Right-click auditions; Ctrl+right-click selects the group to the left of a nearby boundary and exposes Use tag and commit actions; choosing one commits immediately through the same timing transaction. Splitting timed text keeps its old end and inserts a zero-duration right group; a fresh left-side recut can be retimed. Coincident boundaries choose their handle by drag direction. Commit assigns the remaining line duration to one pending slot and zero to subsequent slots. Original line start/end bound the plan; lead commands change these bounds. Karaoke syntax uses centiseconds.

The selected default song-sane kana cut attaches small kana, sokuon and long-vowel marks, and joins the applicable o-row + u vowels; it is not 39 Mode's one-tap-per-mora matcher. Manual cuts can separate a held vowel and joins can group several moras. Japanese opening/closing brackets attach directionally, kanji are separate units, spaces/repeat markers are explicit groups. Renderer `<base|reading>` timing operates on the reading while base tags remain opaque. Nested transforms/drawings/unknown overrides remain intact. Empty drawing/comment slots retain distinct raw anchors even when their logical text offset coincides; decomposed voicing marks use the source’s grapheme-based vowel rule. The existing Automation scanner is shared, while its frozen Lua view retains 3.2.2 normalization and omission of empty zero-duration groups.
