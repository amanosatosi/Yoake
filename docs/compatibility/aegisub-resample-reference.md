Critical result: Mangetsu distort corners are normalized local corner pins, not absolute script pixels. Yoake's existing `DistortTool` maps them through shaped bounds and transforms. Both legacy six-slot P1/P2/P3 and eight-slot P1/P2/P3/P0 forms therefore remain unchanged under ordinary resolution scaling; multiplying them as absolute coordinates would corrupt typesetting.
